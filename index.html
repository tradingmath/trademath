Fees and charges rough estimates
Include estimated fees
Off by default. Shows profit after estimated charges in the calculator, and fills in crypto fees when you close a trade.
These are rough estimates only. Real charges depend on your broker or exchange plan, the contract, taxes and rule changes, so your contract note can differ. Use them for planning, not for your accounts or tax.
Stocks and F&O
Brokerage is capped at 0.03% per order for equity intraday and futures, flat for options, and ₹0 for delivery. STT, exchange, SEBI and stamp duty use the published NSE rates (F&O STT as revised 1 Apr 2026).
Crypto typical defaults, not any one exchange's. Check your exchange's fee page.
Your past trades
