Contact us

We read every message

The quickest way to reach us is email:

support@trademath.in

What to write about

  • Bugs: what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Tell us your browser and device.
  • Ideas and feedback: what would make TradeMath more useful for you.
  • Account or data requests: use the email you signed in with. You can also delete your account yourself in Settings.
  • Wrong figures: lot sizes, fee rates and margins change. If something is out of date, tell us.

Please do not send passwords, one-time codes or broker login details. We will never ask for them.

Response time

We are a small team and usually reply within a few working days. We cannot give trading or investment advice.
