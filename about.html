About TradeMath

Calculator + trade journal for Indian traders

TradeMath helps you decide how much to risk before you enter a trade, and keeps a clear record of how your trades turned out. It is built for traders in India: NSE stocks, F&O in lots, MCX and crypto futures.

What it does

  • Works out position size, stop-loss, targets, margin and risk-reward from your capital and risk per trade.
  • Keeps a trade journal with profit and loss, margin used, mistakes, setups and reports.
  • Gives optional, rough fee estimates, which you can turn on or off.

Free, and your data stays yours

You can use everything without an account. Your journal stays in your own browser unless you choose to sign in for cloud sync. See the privacy page for the details.

Not financial advice

TradeMath is a calculation aid. Figures are estimates, not investment advice. Check everything with your broker before you trade. Trading carries a risk of loss.
