Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

TradeMath is a position-size calculator and trade journal. It has no accounts and no sign-up. This page explains what happens to your information.

Your data stays on your device

  • Your trades, settings, checklist, beep settings and projection inputs are saved in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us or to any server.
  • We cannot see, recover or delete your data. If you clear your browser's site data, switch browser or switch device, it is gone. Use Settings → Your data → Download backup to keep a copy.
  • Backup files, CSV exports and restores are created and read entirely inside your browser.

What we collect

  • TradeMath does not collect, sell or share personal information, and does not use cookies or analytics of its own.
  • The website is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operations.
  • The fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives a normal font request from your browser.

If this changes

If we later add advertising, analytics or affiliate links, those services may use cookies or similar technology, and this page will be updated before they go live.

Not financial advice

TradeMath is a calculation aid. Results, including margin, lot-size, fee and projection figures, are estimates and can be wrong or out of date. They are not investment advice and not a guarantee of any outcome. Check everything with your broker before you trade. Trading carries a risk of loss.

Children

TradeMath is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
