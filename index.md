# TradingMath: position size calculator and trade journal for Indian traders

TradingMath is a free position size calculator and trade journal. Enter your entry, stop-loss, capital and risk to get quantity, margin, targets and estimated charges for NSE stocks, F&O lots, MCX and crypto futures. Then log the trade and review your results. No sign-up is needed. Your journal stays in your browser unless you choose to sign in for cloud sync.

## What it does
- Position size from risk: quantity, stop-loss, targets, risk-reward and margin
- NSE F&O lot sizes, options and futures, and crypto perpetuals in USDT
- Optional, rough fee estimates (off by default)
- Trade journal with profit and loss, margin used, calendar, reports and mistakes
- Planner: a what-if simulator for win rate and risk-reward (an illustration, not a forecast)
